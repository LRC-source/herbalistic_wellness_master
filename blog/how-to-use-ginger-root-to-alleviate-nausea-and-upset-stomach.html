Ways To Use Ginger Root

Ginger is a dependable natural remedy that has been shown to be effective in reducing nausea and vomiting in various studies but knowing how to harness it powers is the first step. Here are some ways to use ginger to alleviate your stomach issues.

  1. Ginger tea: One of the easiest ways to use ginger is to make ginger tea. Simply slice some fresh ginger root and steep it in boiling water for about 10 minutes. You can also add some honey or lemon juice to enhance the flavor.
  2. Ginger capsules: If you don't like the taste of ginger, you can take ginger capsules instead. Capsules are made from dried ginger root powder and are available in health food stores and online. Follow the instructions on the label for dosage.
  3. Ginger chews: Ginger chews are another convenient way to get the benefits of ginger. These are small candies made from ginger root and can be found in health food stores or online. They're easy to carry around and can be eaten anytime you feel nauseous or have an upset stomach.
  4. Ginger in cooking: You can also add ginger to your cooking to reap its digestive benefits. Grate some fresh ginger root and add it to stir-fries, soups, or marinades. Ginger pairs well with fish, chicken, and vegetables, and can add a spicy kick to your meals.

Whether you prefer to drink ginger tea, take ginger capsules, or chew on ginger candies, there are many ways to incorporate this amazing herb into your diet. Try some of these methods the next time you experience digestive discomfort and let nature inspire you.
