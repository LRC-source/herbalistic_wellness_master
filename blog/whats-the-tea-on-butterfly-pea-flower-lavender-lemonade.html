Butterfly Pea Flower Lavender Lemonade

Welcome to the world of butterfly pea flower lavender lemonade! This refreshing and delicious beverage is a unique twist on the classic lemonade. It combines the sweet and tart flavors of lemonade with the calming and soothing properties of lavender and the vibrant blue color of butterfly pea flowers. This drink is perfect for a hot summer day, or for a special occasion. It's easy to make and can be enjoyed by everyone. So, let's get started and learn how to make this delicious and beautiful drink!

How to Make Refreshing Butterfly Pea Flower Lavender Lemonade

Welcome to the wonderful world of butterfly pea flower lavender lemonade! This refreshing and unique beverage is sure to be a hit at your next gathering. Here's how to make it:

Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons of dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 2 tablespoons of dried lavender
  • 2 cups of boiling water
  • 1/2 cup of freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup of honey
  • 2 cups of cold water

Instructions:

  1. In a medium-sized bowl, combine the butterfly pea flowers and lavender.
  2. Pour the boiling water over the mixture and let it steep for 10 minutes.
  3. Strain the mixture into a pitcher and discard the flowers and lavender.
  4. Add the lemon juice, honey, and cold water to the pitcher and stir until the honey is dissolved.
  5. Serve over ice and enjoy!

This butterfly pea flower lavender lemonade is sure to be a hit with your friends and family. It's a unique and refreshing beverage that's perfect for any occasion. Enjoy!
