Powerhouse Herbs For Nerve Regeneration

By LaToya Renee, The Herbalist • Mar 25, 2026

An Herbalist Point Of View

The human nervous system is a complex network of nerves that control and regulate bodily functions, movement, and sensory experiences. It is responsible for transmitting information between the brain and various parts of the body, allowing us to move, think, feel and react to our environment. When the nervous system is damaged or compromised, it can result in conditions such as paralysis, chronic pain, and neuropathy.

Fortunately, there are a number of natural remedies that can help with nerve regeneration and repair. In this article, let's explore:

Seven Powerhouse Herbs Scientifically Proven To Promote Nerve Regeneration

  1. Ashwagandha

Ashwagandha is an adaptogenic herb that has been used in Ayurvedic medicine for centuries. It is known for its ability to reduce stress and anxiety, boost energy levels, and improve cognitive function. But did you know that ashwagandha can also help with nerve regeneration?

Studies have shown that ashwagandha can promote the growth of nerve cells and improve nerve function in people with nerve damage. This is thought to be due to the herb’s ability to reduce inflammation and oxidative stress in the body.

Ashwagandha is available in supplement form and can be taken daily to support nerve health and regeneration.

  1. Turmeric

Turmeric is a bright yellow spice that is widely used in Indian and Middle Eastern cuisine. It is also a powerful anti-inflammatory and antioxidant that has been shown to have a positive effect on nerve health.

Studies have found that turmeric can help to regenerate damaged nerves and improve nerve function in people with conditions such as peripheral neuropathy. This is thought to be due to the herb’s ability to reduce inflammation and protect the nerves from oxidative stress.

Turmeric can be consumed in supplement form or added to meals as a spice. However, it is important to note that turmeric can interact with certain medications, so it is important to talk to a healthcare professional before taking it as a supplement.

Ginkgo biloba is available in supplement form and can be taken daily to support nerve health and regeneration.
