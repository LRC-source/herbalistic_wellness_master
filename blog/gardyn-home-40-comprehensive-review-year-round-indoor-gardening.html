Gardyn Home Features & Benefits :

  • LED Grow Lights: The system incorporates full-spectrum LED grow lights that mimic natural sunlight, ensuring optimal plant growth even in low-light conditions.
  • Automatic Watering and Fertilizing: The Gardyn 4.0 takes the guesswork out of plant care with its automated watering and fertilizing system. You'll receive notifications when it's time to refill the water tank or add plant food, ensuring your plants receive consistent care.
  • Kelby, the AI-Powered Gardening Assistant: Kelby is your virtual gardening companion, offering personalized guidance and support throughout your growing journey. From plant selection and care tips to troubleshooting and harvest reminders, Kelby is there to help you succeed.
