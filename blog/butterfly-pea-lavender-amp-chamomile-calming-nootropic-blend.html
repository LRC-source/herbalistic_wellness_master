What's The Tea?

Let's start with the butterfly pea flower, which is widely known for its exceptional antioxidant properties. Antioxidants play a crucial role in protecting your body from the harmful effects of free radicals, helping to reduce inflammation and support overall health. By incorporating this potent ingredient into our tea blend, we aim to provide you with a natural and effective way to safeguard your body against oxidative stress.

Moving on to lavender and chamomile, these two remarkable herbs have been celebrated for centuries due to their soothing and calming effects. Lavender is known for its aromatic scent, which can help ease tension, reduce anxiety, and support relaxation. Meanwhile, chamomile is renowned for its gentle sedative properties, promoting a sense of tranquility and aiding in stress relief. By combining these two botanical powerhouses, we have created a blend specifically designed to help you unwind, destress, and find inner peace in the midst of a hectic day.

The Blue Star Of The Show

Our first signature blend offers a range of benefits that extend beyond its taste. One of the key ingredients, the Butterfly Pea flower, provides nootropic benefits, which can improve mental clarity and focus. Nootropics are natural substances known for supporting cognitive function, enhancing memory, concentration, and overall brain health. By incorporating our Butterfly Pea, Lavender, & Chamomile Calming Nootropic Tea Blend into your routine, you can enjoy a cup of tea that not only relaxes your mind but also enhances your mental acuity, allowing you to remain productive and focused throughout the day.

Furthermore, this versatile blend offers additional benefits to support your overall well-being. It can be used as a sleep aid, helping to promote a restful night's sleep and rejuvenating your body and mind. The digestive aid properties of this tea blend can also assist in soothing and improving digestion, helping you maintain a healthy gut and overall digestive wellness.
