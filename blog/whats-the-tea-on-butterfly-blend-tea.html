History of Butterfly Blend Tea

Butterfly Blend teas originate from Thailand, where it has been enjoyed for many centuries. The tea is made from the butterfly pea flower, which has been used in traditional Thai medicine for its many health benefits. In Thai culture, the butterfly pea flower is also known for its ability to enhance memory and concentration.

Over time, Butterfly Blend teas has become popular in other parts of the world, particularly in Asia and Europe. Today, Butterfly Blend teas can be found in many specialty herb shops and online stores, making it easily accessible to tea lovers around the globe.

Ingredients in Butterfly Blend Tea

The main ingredient in Butterfly Blend teas are the butterfly pea flower, which gives the tea its vibrant blue color. Ways to Consume Butterfly Blend Teas

Butterfly Blend tea can be consumed hot or cold, depending on your preference. To make hot tea, simply steep a tea bag or loose tea leaves in hot water for 3-5 minutes. For cold tea, you can either brew the tea and let it cool in the fridge or make iced tea by steeping the tea in hot water and then pouring it over ice.

If you're feeling creative, you can also use Butterfly Pea Flower as a natural food dye. The vibrant blue color of the tea can be used to color everything from baked goods to smoothies. And remember, when lemon juice is added, the tea changes color to a beautiful shade of purple!
