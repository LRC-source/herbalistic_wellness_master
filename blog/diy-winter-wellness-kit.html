What's a Tincture?

Before we dive in, let's talk about tinctures. Think of them as liquid extracts of herbs—they're concentrated doses of an herb's goodness in a bottle. I create mine by soaking herbs in 100% grain alcohol for several weeks, which pulls out all the potent properties that you can't get from just a tea. They are a simple and convenient way to take your herbs, and they're perfect for a busy, on-the-go lifestyle.

To use a tincture, you simply add a few drops to water, juice, or your favorite herbal tea. You can also add take a dropper full under the tongue ( hold 30 seconds then swallow) for direct delivery. It's a quick, easy, and effective way to get your daily dose of herbal support.

The Star Players for Your Wellness Kit

Elderberry Tincture for Immune Support Elderberry has been treasured for centuries for its powerful ability to support the immune system. It's packed with antioxidants that help the body stay resilient and strong. As we head into fall and winter, Elderberry Tincture is your number one ally for staying rooted in health.

Ginger Tincture for Digestive & Anti-inflammatory Support Ginger is a true powerhouse. This warming and invigorating herb is a natural anti-inflammatory that helps reduce swelling and discomfort. It's also a champion for digestive support, helping to calm an upset stomach and ease bloating. A few drops of Ginger Tincture can be a game-changer for your daily wellness ritual.

Echinacea Tincture: When you feel your body needs a little extra support, especially as the seasons shift, Echinacea Tincture is your go-to ally. For centuries, this beautiful purple flower has been revered for its ability to support the immune system and help your body maintain its natural balance. My tincture is a potent, handcrafted extract designed to be a convenient part of your daily routine. It's a supportive ritual that reminds you to listen to your body and give it the nurturing it needs to stay resilient all year long.

Mullein Leaf for Throat & Lungs When your throat feels scratchy and your lungs need a little extra love, Mullein Leaf is where it's at. This herb has been traditionally used to soothe coughs, clear congestion, and calm irritation in the respiratory system. A simple tea made from this soft, downy leaf is a wonderful way to help you breathe easier and feel more comfortable.

Nerve, Joint, and Pain Salve for Targeted Relief To round out your kit, we have my Nerve, Joint, and Pain Salve. This is a true labor of love, formulated with a blend of 12 powerful herbs. As the weather gets cooler, our bodies can feel a little achy.
