The Alluring Healthy Delights of Butterfly Pea Flower Tea!

By LaToya Renee, The Herbalist • Mar 12, 2025

By La'Toya Renée The Herbalist

Hey there! Have you ever heard of delights of Butterfly Pea Flower tea, also known as blue tea? It's a fantastic caffeine-free herbal drink made by brewing the flower petals or whole flowers of the Clitoria ternatea plant. Clitoria ternatea goes by various names like butterfly pea, blue pea, Aprajita, Cordofan pea, Blue Tea Flowers, or Asian pigeonwings.

What makes this tea so unique is its captivating deep blue color, which comes from the petals. This beautiful hue has been used as a natural dye for centuries! Known for being rich in antioxidants this delightful tea has more to offer than just its beautiful appearance and some antioxidants. So, why not explore the world of Butterfly Pea Flower Tea and add a splash of color and goodness to your teacup? Cheers to a healthier and more vibrant you!

Butterfly Pea Flower Benefits

Butterfly Pea Flower Tea is not just a visually stunning blue tea, but it also has a lot of health benefits. Here are some of the benefits of drinking Butterfly Pea Flower Tea:

  1. Rich in Antioxidants: Butterfly Pea Flower is loaded with antioxidants, this helps to eliminate free radicals from the body and protect it from oxidative stress.
  2. Promotes Healthy Skin: The antioxidants present in Butterfly Pea Flower Tea also help in maintaining healthy skin. It helps to reduce wrinkles, fine lines, and skin aging.
  3. Boosts Brain Function: This tea contains flavonoids that can improve brain function and boost memory. It is also known to reduce anxiety and stress levels.
  4. Helps in Digestion: Butterfly Pea Flower Tea can help in digestion and prevent constipation. It also has anti-inflammatory properties that can relieve stomach discomfort and bloating.
  5. Lowers Blood Pressure: Regular consumption of Butterfly Pea Flower Tea can help to lower blood pressure levels.
  6. Supports Immune System: The tea is rich in Vitamin C and other essential nutrients that support the immune system and improve overall health.
  7. No Caffeine: Unlike other tea varieties, Butterfly Pea Flower Tea is caffeine-free, making it an excellent choice for those who want to avoid caffeine.

In addition to these benefits, other delights of Butterfly Pea Flower Tea are it's unique taste and aroma. You'll find that Butterfly Pea flower has a light delicate floral flavor that can be enjoyed hot or cold. It's perfect for sipping on a lazy afternoon or as a refreshing drink during a hot summer day.
