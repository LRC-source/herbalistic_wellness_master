The Many Benefits and Uses of Gingko Biloba : What's The Tea?

By LaToya Renee, The Herbalist • Apr 06, 2026

An Herbalistic Point Of View

Let's sip some tea on the many benefits and uses of Gingko Biloba.

Gingko Biloba is a popular herbal supplement that is known for its many health benefits. This powerful herb has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to help treat a wide range of ailments. It is commonly used to support cognitive function, improve circulation, and reduce inflammation.

What is Gingko Biloba?

Gingko Biloba is a type of tree that is native to China. The tree has been around for millions of years and is one of the oldest species of trees in the world. The tree's leaves and seeds are used to create a variety of supplements and herbal remedies. Gingko Biloba contains several compounds that are believed to be responsible for its many health benefits, including flavonoids and terpenoids.

Benefits of Gingko Biloba

Cognitive Function

One of the most well-known benefits of Gingko Biloba is its ability to support cognitive function. This herb has been shown to improve memory, focus, and overall cognitive performance in both young and elderly adults. It does this by increasing blood flow to the brain and improving oxygen uptake.

    Reduce Inflammation

    Gingko Biloba has powerful anti-inflammatory properties, making it an effective treatment for conditions such as arthritis and asthma. It can also help to reduce inflammation in the brain, which is believed to be a contributing factor in the development of neurodegenerative diseases.

      Improve Circulation

      Gingko Biloba can help to improve circulation throughout the body. This can be especially beneficial for individuals with conditions such as Raynaud's disease, where blood flow to the extremities is restricted. By increasing circulation, Gingko Biloba can help to reduce symptoms such as numbness and tingling.

        Uses of Gingko Biloba

        Memory Support

        Biloba is often used as a memory support supplement. It has been shown to improve short-term memory and can be helpful for individuals experiencing mild cognitive decline.

          Eye Health

          Gingko Biloba can also be used to support eye health. It has been shown to improve blood flow to the eyes and can be helpful for individuals with conditions such as glaucoma.

          Anxiety and Depression

            Gingko Biloba has been shown to be effective in reducing symptoms of anxiety and depression. It does this by increasing blood flow to the brain and promoting the release of neurotransmitters such as serotonin and dopamine.

              Recipes for Proper Preparation of Gingko Biloba

              Gingko Biloba Tea

              To make Gingko Biloba tea, start by boiling 2 cups of water. Add 1 teaspoon of dried Gingko Biloba leaves to the water and allow it to steep for 10 minutes. Strain the tea and add honey or lemon to taste.

                Gingko Biloba Tincture

                To make a Gingko Biloba tincture, combine 1 part Gingko Biloba leaves with 5 parts alcohol such as vodka or brandy. Allow the mixture to sit for 2-4 weeks, shaking it daily. After 2-4 weeks, strain the mixture and store it in a dark, cool place. To use, add 10-20 drops of the tincture to a glass of water or juice.
