Shining a Light on Multiple Sclerosis: "Understanding and Raising Awareness During MS Awareness Month"

By LaToya Renee, The Herbalist • May 12, 2026

Herbalistic Wellness is a proud supporter of The national Multiple Sclerosis Society and March is Multiple Sclerosis (MS) Awareness Month – a time to shine a light on this often misunderstood and challenging condition. MS is a chronic autoimmune disease that affects the central nervous system, causing a range of symptoms that can vary greatly from person to person.

While there is no known cure for MS, there are many treatment options available to help manage symptoms and improve quality of life. However, a lack of awareness and understanding of the disease can lead to delays in diagnosis, inadequate treatment, and social stigma for those living with MS.

This MS Awareness Month, let's take a closer look at the facts about MS and what we can do to raise awareness and support those living with this condition. MS affects an estimated 2.8 million people worldwide, with women more commonly affected than men. Symptoms can include fatigue, muscle weakness and spasms, cognitive impairment, vision problems, and bladder and bowel dysfunction.

While the exact cause of MS is unknown, research suggests that a combination of genetic and environmental factors may play a role. MS can be difficult to diagnose, as symptoms can mimic those of other conditions. However, early diagnosis and treatment can help slow the progression of the disease and improve outcomes.

Raising awareness and understanding of MS is crucial in improving outcomes for those living with the condition. By educating ourselves and others about MS, we can help reduce stigma and promote early diagnosis and access to effective treatment.

This MS Awareness Month, consider ways to get involved and show your support for those living with MS. This can include wearing orange, the official color of MS Awareness Month, sharing your story or the stories of loved ones affected by MS on social media, or donating to MS research and support organizations. Let's work together to shine a light on MS and support those like myself living with this challenging condition. Together, we can make a difference.
