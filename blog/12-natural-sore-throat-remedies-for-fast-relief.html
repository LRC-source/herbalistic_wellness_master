12 Natural Sore Throat Remedies for Fast Relief

By LaToya Renee, The Herbalist • Nov 25, 2025

A sore throat can be a real pain, making it difficult to swallow, talk, and even breathe. Whether it's caused by a cold, flu, or allergies, a sore throat can be a frustrating and uncomfortable experience. While over-the-counter medications can provide temporary relief, they often come with unwanted side effects. Luckily, there are many natural remedies that can help soothe a sore throat and promote healing without any negative side effects. In this article, we'll explore 12 natural sore throat remedies that can provide fast relief and get you back to feeling your best.

Why Natural Remedies?

Natural remedies for sore throat have been used for centuries and are often preferred over traditional medications due to their healing properties and lack of side effects. These remedies use natural ingredients that are easily accessible and can be found in most homes. They are also often more affordable than over-the-counter medications, making them a popular choice for those looking for healing solutions without breaking the bank.

Herbal Teas for Sore Throat Remedies

Herbal teas are a popular choice for soothing a sore throat. They are made from natural ingredients and can provide fast relief. Some of the best herbal teas for a sore throat include:

  • Chamomile tea: Chamomile has anti-inflammatory properties that can help reduce swelling and pain in the throat.
  • Ginger tea: Ginger has natural antibacterial properties that can help fight off infections and soothe a sore throat.
  • Licorice root tea: Licorice root has been used for centuries to treat sore throats due to its anti-inflammatory and antiviral properties.

Salt Water Gargle

Gargling with salt water is a simple and effective way to soothe a sore throat. The salt helps to draw out excess fluid from the throat, reducing swelling and pain. To make a salt water gargle, mix 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon of salt in a glass of warm water and gargle for 30 seconds before spitting it out. Repeat this several times a day for best results.

Honey

Honey is a natural antibacterial and anti-inflammatory agent that can help soothe a sore throat. It also has a thick consistency that can help coat and protect the throat. Add a spoonful of honey to your herbal tea or mix it with warm water and lemon for a soothing drink.

Steam Inhalation

Inhaling steam can help to loosen mucus and soothe a sore throat. You can do this by filling a bowl with hot water and leaning over it with a towel over your head to trap the steam. You can also add a few drops of essential oils like eucalyptus or peppermint for added relief.

Apple Cider Vinegar

Apple cider vinegar has natural antibacterial properties that can help fight off infections and soothe a sore throat. Mix 1-2 tablespoons of apple cider vinegar with warm water and honey for a soothing drink. You can also gargle with a mixture of apple cider vinegar and warm water to help reduce inflammation and pain.

Marshmallow Root

by Daniel Dan (https://unsplash.com/@outsideclick)

Marshmallow root has been used for centuries to treat sore throats due to its anti-inflammatory and antibacterial properties. It can help soothe a sore throat and reduce swelling. You can make a tea with marshmallow root by steeping 1-2 teaspoons of dried root in a cup of hot water for 10-15 minutes.

Slippery Elm

Slippery elm is a natural demulcent, meaning it can help coat and soothe the throat. It also has anti-inflammatory properties that can help reduce pain and swelling. You can make a tea with slippery elm by steeping 1-2 teaspoons of dried bark in a cup of hot water for 10-15 minutes.

Echinacea

Echinacea is a popular herb known for its immune-boosting properties. It can help fight off infections and reduce inflammation in the throat. You can take echinacea in supplement form, as a tincture, or make a tea with the dried herb.

Peppermint

Peppermint has natural antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties that can help soothe a sore throat. It also has a cooling effect that can help reduce pain and discomfort. You can make a tea with peppermint leaves or add a few drops of peppermint oil to hot water for a soothing drink.

Licorice Root

Licorice root has been used for centuries to treat sore throats due to its anti-inflammatory and antiviral properties. It can help reduce swelling and pain in the throat. You can make a tea with licorice root by steeping 1-2 teaspoons of dried root in a cup of hot water for 10-15 minutes.

Homeopathic Remedies

Homeopathic remedies for sore throat use natural ingredients to stimulate the body's natural healing process. Some popular homeopathic remedies for sore throat include:

  • Belladonna: This remedy is often used for sore throats that come on suddenly and are accompanied by a fever.
  • Hepar sulphuris: This remedy is often used for sore throats that are accompanied by a dry, hacking cough.
  • Mercurius solubilis: This remedy is often used for sore throats that are accompanied by swollen glands and a thick, yellow mucus.

Rest and Hydration

by Sandra Seitamaa (https://unsplash.com/@seitamaaphotography)

One of the best natural remedies for a sore throat is rest and hydration. Getting plenty of rest allows your body to focus on healing, while staying hydrated helps to thin out mucus and soothe the throat. Make sure to drink plenty of water and other fluids like herbal teas and soups to stay hydrated and promote healing.

When to See a Doctor

While natural remedies can be effective in treating a sore throat, it's important to know when to seek medical attention. If your sore throat lasts longer than a week, is accompanied by a high fever, or is severe and interfering with your daily activities, it's best to see a doctor. They can determine the underlying cause of your sore throat and provide appropriate treatment. Visit the Mayo Clinic for more information on sore throats and the best time to see your doctor.

Takeaways

Sore throats can be a frustrating and uncomfortable experience, but there are many natural remedies that can provide fast relief and promote healing. From herbal teas and salt water gargles to homeopathic remedies and rest, these natural solutions can help soothe a sore throat without any negative side effects. If your sore throat persists or is severe, make sure to seek medical attention for proper diagnosis and treatment. With these natural remedies, you can get back to feeling your best in no time.
