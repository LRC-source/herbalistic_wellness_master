5 Herbal Remedies for Stress Relief

By LaToya Renee, The Herbalist • Dec 22, 2023

Hey Herbies, welcome to another Wellness Wednesday! Today, we're going to talk about stress and how herbs can help you manage it. Stress is a common problem that can have negative effects on your health and well-being.

Stress is a natural physical and psychological response to challenging situations or events that require a response from the body. While some stress can be beneficial, chronic or excessive stress can have negative effects on the body and increase the risk of certain diseases.

When a person experiences stress, their body releases stress hormones such as cortisol and adrenaline. These hormones cause a range of physiological changes, including increased heart rate, faster breathing, and elevated blood pressure. In the short-term, these changes can help us respond to a threat or danger by increasing our alertness and energy.

However, if stress becomes chronic, these physiological changes can have negative effects on the body. Chronic stress can contribute to a range of health problems, including:

  1. Cardiovascular disease: Elevated levels of stress hormones can contribute to the development of high blood pressure, heart disease, and stroke.
  2. Digestive issues: Chronic stress can cause digestive problems such as ulcers, irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), and acid reflux.
  3. Mental health problems: Stress can contribute to the development or exacerbation of mental health conditions such as anxiety and depression.
  4. Immune system dysfunction: Prolonged stress can weaken the immune system, making it more difficult for the body to fight off infections and illnesses.

In addition to these physical effects, chronic stress can also have negative effects on our overall well-being and quality of life. That's why it's important to prioritize stress management and find healthy ways to cope with stress, such as exercise, meditation, or herbal remedies.

Fortunately, there are many herbal remedies for stress relief and that can help you relax, unwind, and think more clearly . Here are five of our favorites:

  1. Chamomile: Chamomile is a gentle herb that has been used for centuries to promote relaxation and calmness. It's a great herb to use if you're feeling anxious or stressed.
  2. Lavender: Lavender is another soothing herb that is known for its ability to calm the mind and promote relaxation. It's often used in aromatherapy to help reduce stress and improve sleep.
  3. Ashwagandha: Ashwagandha is an adaptogenic herb that can help your body cope with stress. It's been shown to reduce cortisol levels, which is the hormone that is associated with stress.
  4. Lemon Balm: Lemon balm is a member of the mint family and is known for its ability to calm the mind and improve mood. It's also been shown to reduce anxiety and promote better sleep.
  5. Passionflower: Passionflower is a natural sedative that can help you relax and fall asleep more easily. It's also been shown to reduce anxiety and improve mood.
