Privacy Policy

Reverence for your personal sovereignty.

✦ Our Commitment to Privacy

At Herbalistic Wellness, we hold your personal data in the same high reverence as the organic botanical ingredients we formulate. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is kept safe, secure, and confidential.

✦ Information We Collect

We collect only the essential personal details required to provide you with high-performance remedies and courses:

- Apothecary Signups: Name and email addresses collected through our newsletter forms (Flodesk/footer), Herb & Soul Podcast forms, and Course modals.

- Local Cache Credentials: Saved browser credentials (`localStorage`) used strictly to customize your dynamic multi-product checkout orders and display student welcome states.

- Firebase Realtime Database: Verified student profiles and course enrollments are securely logged to verify active sandbox module permissions.

✦ Third-Party Services & Payment Gates

We integrate with verified, secure third-party platforms to orchestrate checkout and content streams:

- Square Checkout: Pushes order line-items securely to Square merchant APIs. We never store, process, or see your raw credit card numbers.

- Google Cloud Run & Analytics: Google Cloud endpoints process dynamic checkout compiler scripts with standard encryption. Google Analytics tracks dynamic SPA pageview events to optimize layout load times.
