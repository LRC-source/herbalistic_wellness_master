Shipping Policy & FAQ

Handcrafted delivery, managed with reverence.

✦ What is your shipping and handling policy?

Every single order is packaged with certified organic, fully biodegradable materials to honor the earth. We process and package remedies on a circadian schedule to maintain optimal constitutional freshness.

- Handling Time: Standard orders are prepared and processed within 2 to 4 business days.

- Shipping Rates: Flat-rate USPS ground shipping is calculated at checkout based on standard merchant variables. Orders over **$75.00** qualify for complimentary ground shipping.

✦ How do I track my showroom purchases?

Once your order has been dispatched from our botanical workspace, you will receive a secure confirmation email carrying a USPS or UPS tracking link. You can also view details of your previous orders through the My Account Dashboard.

✦ Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship physical formulations across the United States and Canada. For international inquiries or custom bulk apothecary shipments, please get in touch with our partner team via the Contact Form.
