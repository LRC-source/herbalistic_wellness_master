Wholesale FAQ

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

We strictly enforce an MOQ of 24 units across all product categories to qualify for wholesale pricing.

What are the shipping timelines?

As all our formulations are small-batch and handcrafted, please allow 7-10 business days for wholesale order processing and fulfillment prior to shipping.

How do I get approved for a Trade Account?

Complete the Trade Account Application with your valid resale license and business details. Our admin team typically reviews applications within 48 hours.

How does White Label Custom Formulation work?

Approved White Label clients (80% margin tier) can purchase our base formulas in bulk for private labeling. Contact our wholesale team directly for details on bespoke formulation adjustments.
